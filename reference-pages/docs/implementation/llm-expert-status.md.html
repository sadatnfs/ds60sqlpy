Two concrete helper repairs#

Inspection found that finite Q/K elements can overflow while forming attention scores. llm-043 now controls numerical warning behavior during that operation and raises ValueError for nonfinite allowed scores. llm-046 now requires a positive head width and uses a checked score helper in dense and online paths. Undefined products and zero-width divisions no longer become accepted NaN outputs. The fixes are aligned in authoring/specs.py, guide helpers and executable references.

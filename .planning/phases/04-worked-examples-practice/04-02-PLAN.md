# Plan 04-02: worked examples, guides and solutions, track by track

## Tasks

One agent per track or track group closes `thin-guide`, `few-examples` and
`thin-solution` for its tracks in the authoring sources, following
`files/p4/rules.md` (session files):

- Add interpreted worked examples (or interpreted non-code artifacts) where a
  guide has fewer than two.
- Extend thin guides with lesson-specific explanation: why the technique
  works, a traced example, a common misconception and its correction.
- Extend thin solutions with per-task reasoning: approach, why it is correct,
  a plausible wrong approach and why it fails, and verification.
- Keep the track's templated-word total from rising.

Groups are assigned from the post-Phase-3 gap table.

- `build_coverage_matrix.py --track <t> --gaps thin-guide,few-examples,thin-solution`
  prints no lessons for each track, and the templated total did not rise.

  normalizer pass.
